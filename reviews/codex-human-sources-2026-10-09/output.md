The integration improves coverage, but **Norman should anchor acquaintance applicability, not π itself**.

1. **Norman’s proxy.** The indifferent peer is self-selected, rather than sampled from a defined population; indifference may itself select particular relationships or personalities. Ratings therefore concern selected acquaintances. The 0–2 scale combines accuracy, intensity and occasional applicability: its mean cannot identify the proportion possessing a trait. Liked/disliked ratings reveal evaluative dependence but don’t necessarily bracket the bias relative to population prevalence; self–peer differences also mix information and perspective with endorsement bias. The 1960s university sample adds historical, demographic and semantic transport assumptions. Norman is a valuable primary **human applicability measure**, including for extreme words, conditional on coverage and comprehension checks. Calling it the “closest” π proxy remains a judgement, not validation. ([Inventory, “Read and verified”](notes/human-data-sources-2026-10-09.md))

2. **Keep constructs apart first.** Report source-specific associations, then consider restricted bridge models on overlapping words. Overlap cannot separate construct, era and population effects when these change together. Partial pooling helps estimation; it doesn’t establish measurement equivalence. Matching model prompts to Norman must preserve peer selection, rather than silently substitute “people generally.” Calibration extends predictions of each source’s responses only within demonstrated support. Critically, Rothbart–Park’s α=.84 does **not** supply per-trait standard errors or establish accuracy. Norman’s SD/√n estimates sampling uncertainty under sampling assumptions, not selection or shared judgement bias. ([Plan, “Display frequency is not prevalence”, “Which rarity”, “Commonness judges”, “Overall warrant”](analysis_plan.md:43))

3. **Extraction needs additional controls.** Two independent passes and range checks are necessary but insufficient: both can share column shifts, decimal errors or sex/task misalignment. Retain page/row provenance; reconcile against scans; audit agreeing rows, especially extremes and negations; preserve unknown-term exclusions and task-specific denominators. Freeze rules for sex aggregation, missingness and rounded printed values. ([Inventory, Norman entry](notes/human-data-sources-2026-10-09.md))

4. **Exposure and omitted safeguards.** Freezing the L1 frame now limits future discretion but cannot restore outcome-naïveté. Log exposed items and every subsequent design choice; report exposed/unexposed sensitivity analyses without presenting the latter as untouched confirmation. Hofstee challenges L1 **conditional on breadth tracking prevalence**; breadth is no direct falsifier. Human word ratings also retain morphology-dependent measurement bias and cannot validate morphology-free description judgements. Expanded extreme-word coverage must pass support checks rather than automatically inherit human warrant. ([Inventory, “Exposure log”; plan, “L1”, “L2”, “Model judges only”](analysis_plan.md:113))
