**Adequate as a risk-reduction protocol, but insufficient to establish blindness.** Keep the repository public; strengthen these points:

- **Measure exposure separately from inference.** Before recruitment, screen neutrally for familiarity with Brett’s relevant work, collaborations and prior discussions. After the last task, collect an open-ended hypothesis guess, confidence, when the idea arose, and any searching or outside discussion. A correct guess doesn’t establish exposure; an incorrect guess doesn’t establish blindness. “Measured blindness” overstates what self-report identifies.

- **Specify contamination decisions beforehand.** Known exposure before coding should trigger replacement for the blind-primary role. For exposure during coding, record timing, retain original responses, and report affected tasks separately. If no unexposed replacement exists, explicitly report that the planned blind-primary evidence is unavailable. Don’t automatically exclude an accurate guess: it could arise from attentive task performance. These rules matter because [analysis_plan.md, “H1 details,” human calibration](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/development/marked-pole-stereotypes/analysis_plan.md:60) privileges the blind coder.

- **Audit leakage and preserve independent codes.** L1’s fillers and session order help, but grammatical negation, repeated concepts and later word ratings can disclose the contrast before act coding. Have an independent person inspect the complete packet for recognizable strata, asymmetric detail and leading examples. Preserve both original act codes; Brett-led adjudication mustn’t overwrite the blind-primary row. See [“L1,” “Descriptions and judges”](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/development/marked-pole-stereotypes/analysis_plan.md:112).

- **Commit-reveal is sound for fixing materials, not proving blindness.** Commit the complete packet, instructions, assignment/randomization rules and version manifest. A genuinely unpredictable file needs no additional salt, but length isn’t entropy. Prefer SHA-256 over an unambiguously encoded bundle containing a secret random nonce, disclosed with the files; randomized hash commitments protect against candidate guessing ([Stanford cryptography notes](https://crypto.stanford.edu/cs355/23sp/lec3.pdf)). Publish only after **all** judges finish. Gitignore isn’t access control.

**Better allocation:** if feasible, use separate naïve judges for comparisons and subsequent coding. Hierarchical modelling cannot identify exposure bias from one coder; Brett/model rows are comparisons, not corrections.
