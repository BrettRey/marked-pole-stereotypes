The diagnosis is right; the fix is necessary but needs qualification.

- **Diagnosis.** [analysis_plan.md, L1, “Descriptions and judges”](/Users/brettreynolds/projects/LLM-CLI-projects/papers/development/marked-pole-stereotypes/analysis_plan.md:110) excludes complements precisely because they can make a negated concept common. That selects meanings partly by their expected prevalence. Given Horn’s supplied passage, a uniform contrary rule risks systematic distortion in the discriminating positive-negated pairs. The passage supports investigating this covariance; it doesn’t establish its magnitude in the frozen frame. “Feature absence” also needs sense-specific qualification.

- **Adequacy.** Freeze readings before commonness judgments, preserve genuine complements, and report both-attested alternatives. However, reading-stratified θ requires **both pair types within each stratum**. Report counts first: sparse or absent overlap defeats that contrast, and partial pooling can’t manufacture identification. Pooled θ combines within-reading contrasts with differences in reading composition; distinguish those explicitly. For exact complements, latent prevalence ordering reduces to whether the base exceeds 50%; the coder’s response still includes uncertainty and ties.

- **Post-treatment and blindness.** Reading could itself be a consequence of lexicalization or marking. Conditioning on it therefore doesn’t identify a causal rarity-to-marking effect. For L1’s explicitly descriptive protocol estimand, it is legitimate construct definition, provided the claim stays descriptive. Blindness reduces expectancy effects; it doesn’t remove semantic selection or causal ambiguity. Coding words first with the **same comparison coder** would also compromise morphology blindness through recognition. Use another person for reading adjudication.

- **Instrument and minimum work.** “Neither X nor un-X” is a useful diagnostic, not a decisive instrument: acceptability may reflect vagueness, contextual thresholds, or pragmatic reinterpretation (memory). Ask whether, **under the supplied frozen senses**, someone could satisfy neither description; pair that with a direct complement-versus-opposite paraphrase judgment. Allow unresolved cases. Model screening can reduce workload: a separate blind human adjudicates ambiguous/disputed cases and audits a prespecified sample of apparently clear cases, balanced across morphology and pair type. Morphological defaults should guide screening, not substitute for evidence.

- **Missing checks.** Complement wording restores semantic fidelity but makes grammatical negation visible; test that residual cue separately. Define “false support” against the correctly rendered concepts: support for the distorted **protocol** can be statistically accurate while supporting the substantive hypothesis falsely.
