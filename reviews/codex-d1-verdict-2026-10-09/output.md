1. **Verdict: defensible operationally, overstated logically.** Exclude the current frequency-only joint model from confirmation, provisionally pending D1c. Say “no usable inference under the tested specification,” rather than “a prevalence indicator is essential for every coefficient.” Sampler failure doesn’t establish structural non-identification or necessity in other models. Scenario 1 supports recovery under favourable assumptions, including known indicator-error SD. ([README, “Fitting model” and “Conditions”](scripts/d1/README.md); [plan, “D1”](analysis_plan.md:106))

2. **The bias mechanism is plausible but incomplete.** Frequency, marking **and production** inform z jointly; contamination needn’t enter through frequency alone. Moreover, conditioning on frequency, which receives positive prevalence and negative h loadings, can induce positive prevalence–h association, hence positive b_z, even with accurately measured prevalence. The direct h→marking and h→production paths separately confound b_m. “The latent absorbs h” therefore isn’t an established decomposition of the observed bias. ([`build_joint`](scripts/d1/d1.py:158); [README, “Scenario 2”](scripts/d1/README.md))

3. **The fixes are sensible sensitivity specifications, not guaranteed remedies.** Model register measurement error explicitly; reliable proxies for three distinct constructs needn’t measure the simulated h adequately. Specify the feedback restriction algebraically: retaining frequency as a rarity-dependent outcome in an ordinary joint likelihood still lets it inform rarity. Cutting that feedback alone also leaves frequency-conditioning bias and direct marking–production confounding. Respect the alternative causal structures already required. ([plan, “H1 details”](analysis_plan.md:60); [“Strand B as one generative model”](analysis_plan.md:31))

4. **Two reporting corrections matter.** The sign warning and disclosure of scenario 3’s failure are right. But b_z is conditional; primary H2 averages over frequency **and marking**, while c_z omits the marking path. D1c must evaluate that actual predictive estimand. Report simulation uncertainty around exclusion rates, and distinguish computational failure from biased, converged inference. ([plan, “H2 details”](analysis_plan.md:14); [summary, “Scenario 2,” “Scenario 3,” and “All metrics”](results/d1/SUMMARY_grid.md))
