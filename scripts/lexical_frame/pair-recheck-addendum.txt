PAIR RUBRIC V2: SOURCE SUPPORT AND READING SCREEN

Use ONLY supplied evidence, including in your reasons. Do not state an etymology, historical derivation, date, back-formation history or language of origin unless it is explicitly in the supplied evidence. General morphological hypotheses may be marked as proposals; no historical assertion may be used as a fact. A rejected string analysis needs no invented historical explanation.

A dictionary's use of 'not', 'lack', 'without' or 'deficient' DOES NOT by itself establish logical complementation. Such words can gloss a contrary. For each proposed sense alignment, separately record whether someone could satisfy neither pole and whether someone could satisfy both, keeping context, time and relevant respect fixed. Return yes/no/undetermined for each. Judge the intended concepts, not the spelling. Use 'complementary' only if the supplied evidence supports exhaustive and exclusive categories: neither=no and both=no. Use 'contrary' only when intermediate/neither cases are supported and both poles cannot hold in the same respect: neither=yes and both=no. Otherwise return 'unresolved' and explain which alternative readings remain possible. Definitions alone can leave this undecided; do not manufacture an answer. These are semantic screens, not prevalence questions. Opposites must also concern a compatible domain: eagerness need not oppose patient endurance.

Preserve every supported alignment as a separate candidate interpretation, but do not count those alignments as extra word pairs. Mere nominal privation is outside the primary adjective-pair estimand. A reading left unresolved stays available under explicit alternative readings; it is not discarded for failing to achieve agreement. This is a source-based recheck under a revised rule; no earlier answers or mechanical detector decisions are supplied.
